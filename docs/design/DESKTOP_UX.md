# Desktop UX

The light compact industrial shell supports Product Master, customer/PO, production history, tracking/QR and QC/delivery navigation. Soft neutral background, white/light panels, precise borders, and dark charcoal text keep tables readable without glare. Tables prioritize identifiers, customer, material, size, quantity, delivery date and status. Tracking actions and history are compact, keyboard reachable and usable at 1280x720.
